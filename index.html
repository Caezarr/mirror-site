Mirror is a macOS workflow assistant that recognizes coarse routine structure and prepares reviewable automation drafts. Observation is visible, minimized, and stored locally on your Mac.

Product principles

Useful without becoming surveillance.

01

Local by default

Workflow evidence stays in encrypted storage on your Mac. Mirror has no analytics or advertising pipeline.

02

Minimized before storage

No screenshots, keystrokes, page text, form values, passwords, cookies, query strings, or tokens.

03

User-controlled

Pause observation, exclude private apps or domains, inspect local receipts, and delete retained events.
